ABOUT THE EVENT

A fun 3-round guessing game where Player 1 picks an object from a closed box and helps Player 2 identify it using different methods. Each round becomes more challenging by limiting how the object can be described.
Team Composition
2 Members per team (Player 1 and Player 2)

RULES & REGULATIONS

ROUND 01

Round 1 – Describe It

Player 1 can describe the object, but cannot say its name.

ROUND 02

Round 2 – Act It Out

Player 1 cannot speak and can use only actions and gestures.

ROUND 03

Round 3 – 3 Clues Challenge

Player 1 can give only 3 clues. They cannot:

  • Say the object's name
  • Say its direct use
  • Say its first letter

GENERAL RULES

✓
Each team consists of 2 members: Player 1 and Player 2.
✓
The object will be kept inside a closed box.
✓
Player 1 will pick an object and follow the rules of the particular round.
✓
Player 2 must guess the object within the given time.
✓
No mobile phones, internet, or outside assistance are allowed.
✓
Correct guesses earn points.
✓
The team with the highest score wins.
⚖️
FINAL RULE
The organizer's decision will be final in case of any dispute.
