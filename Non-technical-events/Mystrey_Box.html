Mystery Box

About the Event

A surprise-filled challenge where participants use creativity, logic and teamwork to solve tasks hidden inside mystery boxes. Teams face unexpected tactile, puzzle-solving, and coordination obstacles concealed within themed mystery containers.

Time & Venue

Date: October 2026

Time: 11.00 AM to 12.30 PM

Venue: Activity & Non-Technical Arena

Event Rules and Guidelines

1. Teams and Structure

  • Participation: Teams of 2 to 3 members.
  • Each team is assigned a sequence of sealed mystery boxes containing unknown items, puzzles, and sensory tasks.

2. Challenge Format

  • Round 1 – Tactile Touch & Guess: Identify mysterious items strictly through touch without peeking.
  • Round 2 – Mystery Puzzle Unlock: Decrypt cipher locks and mechanical puzzles inside the box to retrieve secret tokens.
  • Round 3 – Sudden Death Task: Rapid physical and mental team challenge revealed upon opening the final mystery vault.

3. Time Limit & Scoring

  • Each stage has a strictly enforced timer of 1 to 2 minutes.
  • Points are awarded based on identification accuracy, time elapsed, and completed puzzle tasks.
  • Judges' decisions are final and binding.

General Guidelines

  • Respect the coordinators and follow all given instructions.
  • No cheating, tampering, or interference allowed.
  • Maintain fair play and sportsmanship throughout the event.
  • Judges’ and coordinators’ decisions are final.

Rewards & Recognition

  • Exciting prizes are waiting for participants who showcase exceptional talent and creativity.
