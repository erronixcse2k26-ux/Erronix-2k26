Treasure Hunt

About the Event

An exciting clue-based adventure where teams solve puzzles, follow hidden clues and race to discover the final treasure. Treasure Hunt challenges participants across logical reasoning, navigational prowess, cipher cracking, and rapid campus exploration.

Time & Venue

Date: October 2026

Time: 1.30 PM to 3.30 PM

Venue: Campus Grounds / CSE Block

Event Flow & Rules

  • Form Teams: Teams composed of 3–4 members.
  • Clue Trail: Teams start with an initial riddle at checkpoint 1 and must decipher each clue to locate the next station.
  • Checkpoints: Each checkpoint validator stamps the team card upon riddle verification.
  • Fair Play: Tampering with clues or misleading other teams results in immediate disqualification.
  • Victory: The first team to arrive at the final vault checkpoint with all stamps wins.

General Guidelines

  • Participants must register individually or as part of a team.
  • Follow the instructions provided by the event coordinators.
  • Cheating or unfair play leads to disqualification.
  • Decisions by judges are final and binding.
  • Maintain sportsmanship and respect for fellow participants.

Rewards & Recognition

  • Exciting prizes are waiting for participants who showcase exceptional talent and creativity.
