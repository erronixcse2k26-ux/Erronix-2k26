EVENT OVERVIEW

Treasure Hunt is an exciting team-based challenge that combines riddles, puzzles, reasoning, aptitude, and clue-solving skills. Participants must work together to solve challenges, follow hidden clues, and navigate through different locations to ultimately discover the treasure.

All teams will begin from the same starting location, but each team will be assigned a different route through a set of QR codes, making the hunt challenging and engaging.

TEAM SIZE

Each team must consist of 3 to 4 members.
All team members must participate together throughout the event.
One Device Only: Each team must designate and use only one mobile phone for the entire game.

HOW THE GAME WORKS

01
All teams will assemble at the designated starting location.
02
Each team will be provided with or directed to a starting QR code.
03
There will be five different QR-code paths, with each team following its assigned route.
04
When a team scans a QR code, it will receive two questions/clues:
  • A challenge question
  • A location clue
05
The challenge may be a riddle, puzzle, reasoning question, aptitude problem, or similar task that the team must solve.
06
The location clue will indicate where the next QR code is hidden.
07
The team must physically move to the indicated location and scan the next QR code.
08
This process continues through five levels of the treasure hunt.
09
At the sixth level, the team will receive the final clue leading to the hidden treasure.
10
The team that successfully completes the required challenges and reaches the treasure according to the event rules will complete the hunt.

RULES & REGULATIONS

01
Each team must have 3 to 4 members.
02
One-Mobile-Phone Rule: STRICT Each team is permitted to use only one mobile phone throughout the event.
03
The same phone must be used for scanning all QR codes and accessing the clues.
04
Team Members Staying Together: MANDATORY Team members must stay together while participating in the hunt.
05
Teams must solve the challenge questions using their own knowledge and teamwork.
06
Authorized Device Use Only: Unnecessary use of mobile phones is strictly prohibited. The designated phone may be used only for scanning QR codes and accessing the clues required for the game.
07
No External Sources: PROHIBITED Participants must not use search engines, AI tools, social media, messaging apps, or other external sources to obtain answers.
08
Teams must follow the clues in the order provided and must not intentionally skip any level.
09
Participants must not disturb, move, damage, hide, or tamper with QR codes or clues belonging to other teams.
10
Restricted & Unsafe Areas: SAFETY FIRST Participants must not enter restricted or unsafe areas while searching for QR codes.
11
Participants must follow the instructions given by the event coordinators and volunteers at all times.
12
Any form of cheating, intentional rule violation, or interference with another team may result in disqualification.
13
Teams must complete the hunt within the specified time limit.
14
The decision of the event coordinators regarding rule violations, disputes, and completion of the hunt will be final.

IMPORTANT NOTE

The Treasure Hunt is designed to test:
Teamwork
Logical thinking
Problem-solving ability
Observation
Speed
Coordination
Participants are expected to cooperate with their teammates and maintain fair play throughout the event.
