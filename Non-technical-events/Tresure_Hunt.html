ABOUT THE EVENT

Treasure Hunt transforms the college campus into a massive live-action puzzle map, testing deductive reasoning, agility, and teamwork under time pressure.

Teams receive enigmatic clue cards and cryptic riddles that unlock subsequent checkpoints scattered across the campus grounds. Speed, analytical precision, and collective coordination are paramount to unlocking the final mystery vault before rival teams.

RULES & REGULATIONS

01
Team Composition: Teams must consist of 3 to 4 members. Advance online registration is mandatory. Carry official college ID cards.
02
Unity Rule: MANDATORY Teams must move and travel together at all times. Splitting up members across different campus zones results in immediate disqualification.
03
Fair Play & Marker Protection: Tampering with clues, removing station markers, hiding clue cards, or misleading rival teams leads to immediate expulsion.
04
Campus Safety & Decorum: Running inside classrooms, entering restricted faculty zones, or damaging college property is strictly forbidden.
05
Victory Condition: The winning team is determined strictly by the first verified arrival at the final vault with a 100% stamped and intact clue passport.
06
Judges' & Marshals' Authority: The rulings of station marshals and event coordinators are final, binding, and non-negotiable.
