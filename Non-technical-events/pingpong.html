Ping-Pong-Throw

About the Event

Ping-Pong-Throw is a fun-filled, skill-testing event where participants showcase their accuracy, speed, and coordination. Compete against others in a series of challenges that test your precision in throwing ping-pong balls into designated targets. This event is designed to be thrilling and entertaining for both players and spectators.

Event Rules and Guidelines

1. Teams and Players

  • A total of five (5) teams will participate in the event.
  • Each team shall consist of three (3) members.
  • All teams will be given equal opportunity to participate.

2. Equipment: Balls and Bowls

  • Each team will be provided with twelve (12) ping pong balls.
  • Three (3) bowls filled with water will be placed on a table at a fixed distance.
  • Only the materials provided by the organizers may be used during the event.

3. Throw and Aim Instructions

  • All team members must stand behind the throwing line before starting.
  • Each player will take turns to throw the balls one at a time.
  • Each player must attempt to throw until at least one ball successfully reaches the bowl.
  • The goal is to land the ping pong balls into the water-filled bowls.
  • If a player crosses the throwing line or throws multiple balls together, that throw will not be counted.
  • Balls that bounce out of the bowl will not be considered successful.

4. Time Limit

  • Each team will be given one (1) minute to complete their turn.
  • All throws must be completed within the given time limit.

5. Winning Criteria

  • A throw is considered successful when the ball lands in the bowl and stays inside.
  • The team with the highest number of successful throws within the time limit will be declared the winner.
  • In case of a tie, an additional tiebreaker round may be conducted.
  • The judges’ and coordinators’ decisions are final and binding for all participants.

6. Key Rules to Remember

  • Do not cross the throwing line during your turn.
  • Use only the balls and bowls provided by the organizers.
  • One ball per throw – no multiple throws at once.
  • All balls must reach the bowl within only one pitch (attempt).
  • Each player must throw until at least one ball reaches the bowl.
  • No bouncing or rolling throws unless specified.
  • No extra balls may be taken by players — it is strictly not allowed.
  • Maintain fair play and teamwork throughout the event.

General Guidelines

  • Respect the coordinators and follow all given instructions.
  • No cheating, tampering, or interference allowed.
  • Maintain fair play and sportsmanship throughout the event.
  • Judges’ and coordinators’ decisions are final.
