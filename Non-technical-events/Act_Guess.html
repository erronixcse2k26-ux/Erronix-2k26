Act & Guess

About the Event

An entertaining team challenge that combines acting, observation and quick thinking to identify words, phrases and concepts. Teams communicate silently through non-verbal gestures, creative expressions, and theatrical mime clues against the clock.

Time & Venue

Date: October 2026

Time: 2.00 PM to 3.30 PM

Venue: Seminar Hall / Cultural Auditorium

Teams & Format

  • Each team may have 2 to 3 members.
  • The contest consists of multiple rounds including classic mime, tech terms, and rapid-fire acting.

Rounds

  1. Round 1 – Classic Expressions: Act out everyday phrases, movies, and famous personality traits without speaking.
  2. Round 2 – Tech Charades: Enact computing concepts, programming terms, and digital gadgets non-verbally.
  3. Round 3 – Speed Act (Finals): Rapid-fire clue enactment under strict 60-second time limits.

Rules & Guidelines

  • No verbal clues, whispering, lip-syncing, or pointing to actual objects in the room.
  • Teams are penalized or disqualified if verbal sounds are made during acting.
  • Judges’ decisions regarding time and accuracy are final.

Rewards & Recognition

  • Exciting prizes are waiting for participants who showcase exceptional talent and creativity.
