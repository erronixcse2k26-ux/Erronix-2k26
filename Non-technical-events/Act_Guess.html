ABOUT THE EVENT

Act & Guess puts interpersonal communication, theatrical instinct, and non-verbal coordination to the test in an exhilarating live auditorium setting.

One team member acts out movies, technological concepts, everyday situations, or iconic expressions strictly using silent bodily gestures. Teammates must decode the enactment and call out the accurate answer before the countdown timer expires.

ROUND 1 – BIOSCOPE

Participants must guess the song using the images provided.

ROUND 2 – MISSING WORDS

Participants must identify the song by filling in the missing words from the given lyrics/song.
ROUND 03 7 SONGS • 15 MIN

ROUND 3 – ACT & GUESS

One team member acts out the song.
The person acting cannot talk or sing.
The other teammate must guess the song.

BUZZER RULE

The team that buzzes first gets the opportunity to answer.
If the answer is wrong, the opportunity passes to the other teams.
There is no retry for the team that gave the wrong answer.

WINNERS

🏆 PRIZES & TIE-BREAKER

Points-Based Ranking

The top 3 teams based on their points will win prizes.
If there is a tie, the tie will be settled through a tie-breaker.
