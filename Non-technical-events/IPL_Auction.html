IPL Auction

Non-Technical Strategic Simulation Event — Erronix 2K26

About the Event

A strategic team-based auction experience where participants build their dream IPL squad through bidding, planning and decision-making. Test your cricketing acumen, purse management, analytical bidding tactics, and squad composition balance against competing franchises.

Time & Venue

Date: October 2026

Time: 2.00 PM to 4.00 PM

Venue: Main Seminar Hall / Audi-2

Team Structure & Format

  • Teams of 2 to 3 members.
  • Each team is assigned an IPL franchise with an identical starting virtual purse balance.
  • Auctions follow real-time gavel hammer rules with paddle raises and incremental bids.

Squad Composition & Rules

  • Each team must assemble a playing squad fulfilling mandatory category quotas:
    • Minimum 3 Batsmen
    • Minimum 3 Bowlers
    • Minimum 2 All-rounders
    • Minimum 1 Wicketkeeper
    • Maximum overseas player cap enforced
  • Unsold players may re-enter during the accelerated auction round.
  • Overspending beyond the purse balance leads to points deduction or disqualification.
  • Winners are declared based on aggregate player rating points and budget efficiency.

Rewards & Recognition

  • Exciting prizes are waiting for participants who showcase exceptional talent and creativity.
