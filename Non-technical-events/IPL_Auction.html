ABOUT THE EVENT

IPL Auction puts your cricketing acumen, financial discipline, and bidding tactics on the line as you navigate live auction rounds to build the strongest possible franchise roster.

Every registered franchise begins with an identical virtual wallet balance. With real-time paddle raises, gavel drops, and bidding wars, teams must balance star power against budgetary limits while satisfying strict squad quotas. Winners are decided through a composite evaluation of squad balance, ratings, and remaining purse efficiency.

Mandatory Squad Composition Quotas

RULES & REGULATIONS

01
Franchise Team Size: Teams consist of 2 to 3 members. Advance online registration is mandatory. Carry official college ID cards.
02
Gavel Rule: BINDING Once the auctioneer's hammer falls, the bid is final, committed, and cannot be revoked under any circumstances.
03
Mandatory Squad Composition: Teams must fulfill minimum squad quotas:
  • At least 3 Batsmen
  • At least 3 Bowlers
  • At least 2 All-rounders
  • At least 1 Wicketkeeper
  • Overseas player limits strictly enforced
04
Purse Limit Discipline: Any bid exceeding the team's remaining purse balance will trigger immediate penalties or disqualification.
05
Accelerated Bidding: Unsold player lists will be submitted by teams for the accelerated round at discounted base prices to fill roster gaps.
06
Auctioneer Authority: The auctioneer's judgment regarding paddle raises, tie-breakers, and incremental bids is final and indisputable.
