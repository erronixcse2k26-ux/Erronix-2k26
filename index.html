About ErrONiX 2K26

Empowering Innovation from Error to Excellence

Department of Computer Science and Engineering | University College of Engineering Arni

ErrONiX 2K26 is the flagship National Level Technical Symposium hosted by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at University College of Engineering Arni (A Constituent College of Anna University Chennai). Centered on the vision "Error to Excellence", ErrONiX 2K26 serves as a nationwide platform for aspiring engineers to learn, compete, innovate, and grow.

From high-stakes debugging challenges and creative UI/UX wireframing contests to innovative project expos and research paper presentations, ErrONiX 2K26 fosters technical mastery, analytical problem-solving, and collaborative excellence.
