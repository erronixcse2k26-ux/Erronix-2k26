ABOUT THE EVENT

CyberQuest places competitors in front of real-world cybersecurity challenges, requiring you to analyze network protocols, dissect cryptographic ciphers, and capture digital flags under timed pressure.

From decoding hidden messages to analyzing web vulnerabilities and investigating compromised systems, CyberQuest tests your technical acumen, analytical depth, and practical cybersecurity instincts.

RULES & REGULATIONS

01
Reporting & Verification: Arrive at the cybersecurity lab before scheduled time. Advance online registration is mandatory. Carry official college ID cards.
02
Single Browser Window Protocol: STRICT Participants must complete the challenge in a single browser window/tab. Any attempt to switch tabs or windows results in immediate automated disqualification.
03
Zero Gadget Policy: Electronic devices (smartphones, smartwatches, earplugs, external storage devices) are strictly prohibited inside the lab.
04
Timer Expiry: A live countdown timer is displayed. Participants must submit answers before it expires; upon 00:00, tests submit automatically.
05
Technical Disruption Protocol: In the event of any technical anomaly or system disruption, notify student coordinators immediately for official assistance.
06
Fair Play: No discussion or sharing flags between participants. Respect decisions of the technical leads and coordinators; decisions are final and binding.
