ABOUT THE EVENT

A team-based coding relay where two members collaborate during discussion time and take turns coding.

Speed, accuracy, debugging skills, and teamwork will be tested through two progressively challenging rounds.

RULES & REGULATIONS

01
Team: Each team consists of 2 members.
02
Round 1 – Easy Challenge: Both members get 2 minutes to discuss the given problem. Member 1 then codes for 5 minutes, followed by Member 2 for 5 minutes to continue and complete the solution.
03
Round 2 – Advanced Challenge: Both members get 10 minutes to discuss and plan the solution. Member 1 codes for 10 minutes, after which Member 2 takes over for 10 minutes to debug, continue, and complete the solution.
04
Communication: Members can communicate only during the discussion time.
05
No External Assistance: Internet, mobile phones, AI tools, and external assistance are not allowed.
06
Evaluation: Teams will be evaluated based on correctness, test cases, and completion within the given time.
07
Disqualification: Any malpractice will lead to immediate disqualification.
