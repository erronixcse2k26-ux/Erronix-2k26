ABOUT THE EVENT

TechTalkX provides an esteemed stage for undergraduate and postgraduate technologists to articulate innovative concepts, research discoveries, and prototype demonstrations before a panel of academic and industry specialists.

Participants present their projects and research insights covering next-generation computing trends. The event evaluates technical comprehension, originality of implementation, clarity of delivery, and the ability to defend technical decisions during the live question-and-answer session.

Suggested Domains & Research Themes

Artificial Intelligence & ML Generative AI & LLMs Data Science & Analytics Cybersecurity & Cryptography Cloud & Distributed Systems Quantum Computing Human-Centric Computing Hardware & IoT Architecture Open Emerging Technologies

RULES & REGULATIONS

01
Advance Registration & Submission: Advance online registration is mandatory. Participants must submit their presentation abstract/slides for review prior to the event.
02
Submission Email Format: Send a single email to erronixcse@gmail.com with subject “TechTalkX – Presentation Submission and ID Verification”, attaching scanned college ID card(s) and your presentation slides.
03
Originality: Projects and papers must represent original work created by the participants. Plagiarism or deceptive claims result in disqualification.
04
Working Prototype Required: A working prototype or demonstrable software/hardware model must be prepared for demonstration. Participants must bring their own laptops and necessary peripherals.
05
Time Limit Allocation (Strictly 15 Minutes per Team):
  • 7 Minutes – PPT Presentation
  • 5 Minutes – Live Project Demonstration
  • 3 Minutes – Interactive Q&A with Judges
06
Code of Conduct: Participants must arrive punctually at the venue with official college ID. The decision of the judging panel is final and binding.
