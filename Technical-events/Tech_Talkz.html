TechTalkX

About the Event

A technology-focused speaking event where participants present ideas, trends, innovations and concepts from the world of computing. TechTalkX provides a platform for students to demonstrate research depth, presentation skills, and thought leadership before an esteemed panel of judges.

Event Schedule

  • Date: October 2026
  • Time: 11.00 AM to 1.00 PM
  • Venue: Main Auditorium / Seminar Hall

Event Overview

  • Event Name: TechTalkX
  • Team Size: Solo or maximum of 3 members

Registration & Submission

  • Registration Deadline: Advance Online Registration Mandatory
  • Prerequisite: Participants must submit their presentation abstract/slides for review.
  • Send a single email to erronixcse@gmail.com including:
    • Scanned copy of your college ID card(s) for verification.
    • Your presentation slides or abstract document as an attachment.
  • Use the subject line: “TechTalkX – Presentation Submission and ID Verification”.
  • Individual participants should include their own ID; teams must include all members’ IDs in the same email.

Rules & Regulations

  • Projects must be the original work of the participants.
  • A working prototype must be ready for demonstration.
  • Participants must bring their own laptop and necessary equipment.
  • On the event day, participants should present:
    • A PPT Presentation
    • A Live Demonstration

Event Flow & Time Allocation

  • Total Time per Team: 15 minutes
    • 7 minutes – PPT Presentation
    • 5 minutes – Project Demonstration
    • 3 minutes – Q&A with judges

Rewards & Recognition

  • All participants will receive a Participation Certificate.
  • Winners and Runners-up will be awarded special prizes in recognition of their outstanding performance.

Code of Conduct

  • Participants must report on time to the venue.
  • Maintain professionalism and decorum throughout the event.
  • Any violation of rules may lead to disqualification.

Suggested Domains & Topics

  1. Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  2. GenAI
  3. Data Science & Analytics
  4. Cybersecurity & Networking
  5. Computing Paradigms
  6. Emerging Technologies
  7. Human-Centric Computing
  8. Hardware & Future Tech
  9. Quantum Computing
  10. Any other relevant technical topics
