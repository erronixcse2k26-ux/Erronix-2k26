ABOUT THE EVENT

Paper Presentation provides participants with a platform to present innovative ideas, research findings, and emerging technologies across various engineering domains.

Participants will present their selected topic before a panel of judges, followed by a question-and-answer session.

RULES & REGULATIONS

01
Participants can register individually or as a team of maximum 3 members.
02
The paper must be original and relevant to the selected domain.
03
Plagiarism is strictly prohibited and may lead to disqualification.
04
Each team will get 7 minutes for presentation, followed by 3 minutes for Q&A.
05
Participants must use PPT/PPTX for the presentation.
06
The presentation should be limited to 8–12 slides.
07
Participants must report at the venue at least 15 minutes before their allotted time.
08
Participants should carry a backup copy of their presentation.
09
All team members are expected to participate in the presentation and Q&A.
10
The judges' decision will be final and binding.
