Prompt War

About the Event

An AI prompt engineering challenge where participants compete to create effective prompts and solve creative tasks using generative AI. Participants harness the power of state-of-the-art LLMs and generative systems to craft optimal responses, complex workflows, and creative assets.

Event Schedule

  • Date: October 2026
  • Time: 11.00 AM to 1.00 PM
  • Venue: AI & High Performance Computing Lab

Category & Participation

  • Category/Theme: Generative AI & Prompt Engineering
  • Participation: Individual or Teams of 2

Registration & Prerequisites

  • Registration Process: Online / First-come, first-served.
  • Registration Deadline: Advance Online Registration Mandatory
  • Prerequisite: Familiarity with Generative AI interfaces and prompt strategies.

General Rules

  1. Participants must carry a valid college ID card for verification.
  2. Only prompts and outputs generated during the live event window will be evaluated.
  3. The decision of the technical panel and judges is final and binding.

Specific Rules

  1. Participants will receive complex task descriptions across coding, creative writing, multimodal generation, and logic reasoning.
  2. Tasks must be solved exclusively through precision prompt crafting on the designated AI platform.
  3. Participants must submit both the final output and the sequence of prompts used.
  4. Use of external pre-written scripts or automated API bots is strictly prohibited.
  5. Evaluation is based on output accuracy, constraint satisfaction, prompt brevity, and structured thinking.

Event Flow & Time Allocation

  • Duration: 45–60 minutes.
  • Format: Multi-task challenge (Text, Logic, and Creative AI Generation).

Evaluation Criteria

  • Number of bugs correctly fixed.
  • Time taken to fix the bugs.
  • Ranking is based on more bugs fixation in less time.

Rewards & Recognition

  • All participants will receive a Participation Certificate.
  • Winners and Runners-up will be awarded special prizes in recognition of their outstanding performance.

Code of Conduct

  • Participants must report on time.
  • Maintain professionalism and decorum throughout the event.
  • Any violation of rules may lead to disqualification.
