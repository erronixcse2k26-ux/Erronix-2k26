ABOUT THE EVENT

Prompt War is designed to find the next generation of AI whisperers—engineers who can craft precision prompts, control LLM behavior, and generate exact algorithmic and multimodal results.

Participants are presented with multi-tier tasks requiring creative storytelling, exact code generation, algorithmic bug elimination, and strict constraint adherence. With no pre-written code permitted, success hinges on structured few-shot prompting, persona steering, reasoning chains, and minimizing token waste.

RULES & REGULATIONS

01
Eligibility & Verification: Open to individuals or teams of up to 2 members. Participants must present a valid college ID card. Advance online registration is mandatory.
02
Platform Isolation: All tasks must be performed and solved solely within the authorized AI platform provided at the lab. Use of unauthorized external APIs or web tabs is prohibited.
03
No Pre-written Scripts: PROHIBITED Use of external pre-written scripts, automation bots, or pre-saved prompt collections is strictly forbidden.
04
Mandatory Prompt Logs: Submissions must include both the final output and the sequence of prompts utilized. Omission of prompt logs results in score deduction.
05
Evaluation & Scoring:
  • Output fidelity, accuracy, and test scenario coverage.
  • Precision in satisfying edge-case constraints and schemas.
  • Prompt elegance, token efficiency, and elapsed time.
06
Code of Conduct: Participants must arrive on time with official college ID. Maintain quiet and professionalism in the HPC lab. The decisions of the judging panel are final.
