Paper Presentation

About the Event

The Paper Presentation event encourages participants to research, innovate, and present their ideas before a panel of experts. It provides a platform to showcase technical knowledge, research skills, and creativity. Students can participate individually or as a team (1–3 members).

Paper Presentation Rules

  1. Presentation Time: 10 minutes total
    • 7 minutes for presenting
    • 3 minutes for Q&A
  2. Topics must be from the suggested list or relevant interdisciplinary topics with prior approval.
  3. Paper Length: The paper should be between 4 to 6 pages in total.
  4. Submission Format:
    • Papers must follow the IEEE format.
    • PPT must be in 16:9 ratio.
    • Both hard and soft copies of the accepted paper and PPT must be submitted on the event day.
  5. Final PPT must be shared with event coordinators before 30/10/2025.
  6. A pen drive for backup is recommended (not mandatory).
  7. Papers must be original — plagiarism will lead to immediate disqualification.
  8. Download the IEEE format templates here:
    • IEEE Format – Word (.docx)
    • IEEE Format – PDF (.pdf)
